Vendored Framework
==================

This repository vendors the Nixfied framework under `nixfied/`.

Framework source revision (install/upgrade):
- 4f78f424ffef7feb91efc5a01198667bba6e3e0d

Framework-owned (overwritten on framework::upgrade):
- flake.nix, flake.lock
- nixfied/.framework/

User-owned (preserved on framework::upgrade by default):
- nixfied/project/ (primary customization surface)
- nixfied/local/ (extensions: extra apps/packages/devShells)

If you need to customize behavior, prefer editing files under nixfied/project/
and nixfied/local/ rather than editing framework code.
